# Shapi — Competitor Landscape, Pricing & Models

> Living reference. Last updated **21 May 2026**. Sources at the bottom.
> Sister doc: **STRATEGY.md** (our ideas, decisions, positioning).

Shapi sits at the intersection of two established categories. We are **not "first"** at AI
chat or voice interviewing — those are now table stakes. Our edge is the **combination**:
independent reference *sourcing* + AI cross-check + two-sided trust, in a candidate-owned,
portable verified profile, MENA / blue+white collar / WhatsApp-first.

---

## 1. The competitor map (two axes)

### A. AI matching / voice agents (crowded — we are NOT first here)
| Company | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Jack & Jill** (jackandjill.ai) | AI agent "Jack" interviews candidates by voice/chat; reverse-introduces to hiring managers, no application. "Jill" works the employer side. Raised $20M (Oct 2025). London. |
| **HeyMilo** | Conversational AI voice interviews + scoring for employers. ATS integration, multilingual. NY, 2023. |
| **Apriora / "Alex AI"** | Autonomous AI interviewer over video/voice, structured write-ups, ATS sync. YC-backed. |
| **Sapia.ai** | Chat + video AI interviews, bias-reduction / ethical-AI angle, text-based assessments. |
| **Ribbon, ConverzAI** | AI screening / voice interview tools for recruiters. |
| **Mercor** | AI interviews to vet experts (mostly for AI-lab work). |
| **LazyApply, Loopcv, Sonara, AIApply** | Candidate-side auto-apply bots. Low trust (LazyApply 2.4★), blocked by CAPTCHAs. |

### B. Reference checking / verification (also established)
| Company | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Xref** | Automated digital reference checks. Create in ~30s, ~18h turnaround. **Candidate picks referees.** |
| **HiPeople** | AI-powered reference checks + personality/culture/role-fit analytics. |
| **Crosschq, Refapp, SkillSurvey** | Digital reference workflow tools for recruiting teams. |
| **Veremark, Zinc, Checkr, HireRight, Certn** | Background / identity / right-to-work / employment verification (bundle references + screening). |

### C. UAE / regional incumbents
Bayt, GulfTalent, Naukrigulf, Qureos, Dubizzle, Talentmate, Indeed UAE. Blue-collar hiring
today largely runs through **unstructured WhatsApp groups** — a wide-open, low-trust channel.

## 3. Business model — them vs us

| Dimension | Jack & Jill / AI matchers | Reference tools (Xref etc.) | **Shapi** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core job | Match + introduce | Verify references (employer one-off) | **Verify identity + match, two-sided** |
| Who picks referees | n/a | **Candidate** (weak — testimonial reel) | **Shapi sources independently** (candidate can't curate) |

| Region | Generic / US-UK | Generic | **MENA-first, right-to-work intelligence per country** |
| Monetization | Success fee (10%) | SaaS / per-check | **Subscription-first + small placement (Phase 2)**; payments = facilitator |

## 4. Where Shapi is genuinely differentiated
1. **Independent reference SOURCING** — we choose who to contact; candidate doesn't curate answers. (Closest thing to a true "first" — everyone else lets the candidate pick.)
2. **AI cross-check → candidate-owned verified profile.** Others sell verification *to employers* as a one-off; we make it a portable asset the candidate reuses.
3. **Two-sided trust** — company trust score in the match. Matching tools don't do this.
4. **Blue + white collar, phone-first, native language, region right-to-work, UAE-first.**
5. **The moat = proprietary outcome data** (references + cross-checks + company ratings + did-the-hire-stay), which compounds and trains a real fit/quality model nobody else has.

## 5. What we must STOP claiming
- ❌ "First at AI / voice / agent chat." Jack & Jill + many others already do it.
- ✅ Claim instead: **"The verification layer for hiring — independent, two-sided, candidate-owned."**
