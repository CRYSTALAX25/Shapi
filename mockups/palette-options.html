Recommendation

Option A — Violet Mint. Violet owns "intelligence + premium" in a market drowning in HR-tool blue, and ending the brand gradient in verified-green makes the logo itself say what Shapi is: intelligence that resolves into verified truth.

A

Violet Mint

Your purple + green instinct, sharpened. Violet is the brand voice (intelligence, depth, premium); emerald appears only as verified/positive signal — so every green pixel is earned, never decorative. The gradient runs violet → emerald: the product's promise drawn as colour.

★ Recommended

Psychology

Purple = intelligence, judgment, premium — the colour of insight rather than process. Green = growth and proof. Together: "we think, then we verify." Exactly the workforce-intelligence + verification story.

Differentiation

LinkedIn, Workday, Greenhouse, ChartHop, every ATS: blue. A violet-led dark product instantly reads as a different species — an AI-native intelligence layer, not another HR portal.

Accessibility

Violet #9D8CFF on #060609 = 7.3:1 (AA for body text, AAA for large). Emerald #34D399 = 10.5:1. Both safe at chip/label sizes — no contrast compromises anywhere.

Verified green inside it

The cleanest read of all four options. Green is the only warm-positive in a cool violet field, so a verified badge pops instantly. Rule: green is never used for decoration — only verified / success / "do this."

B

Ocean Emerald

Your blue + green instinct. An electric azure — deliberately brighter and more saturated than LinkedIn's corporate #0A66C2 — paired with the same earned emerald. Safe, trustworthy, instantly legible as "credible tech."

Strong runner-up

Psychology

Blue = trust, stability, competence — the most pre-validated trust colour in existence. Blue+green is the "credibility + growth" pairing banks and fintechs use because it works on everyone, everywhere.

Differentiation

The honest weakness. Even electric azure sits in the same mental folder as LinkedIn, Indeed, Workday, Wise. You'd rely on layout and typography to feel different — the colour itself won't do that work.

Accessibility

Azure #4DA6FF on #060609 = 7.9:1, emerald = 10.5:1. Technically the strongest-contrast pairing of the four. Zero issues at any size.

Verified green inside it

Workable but softer than A: blue and green are adjacent hues, so a verified badge pops less against a blue field than against violet. The moat colour loses a little of its punch.

C

Solar Violet

The wildcard: iris violet + warm gold, with green removed from the brand entirely and reserved 100% for verification. Gold speaks fluently to the Gulf premium market; verified-green becomes the single most sacred colour in the product.

Wildcard · premium play

Psychology

Violet + gold = luxury, judgment, prestige — closer to a private bank than an HR tool. In the UAE/KSA market gold carries real cultural weight for "premium, serious, established."

Differentiation

Maximum. No hiring product on the market is violet-and-gold. Reads as an executive intelligence product — perfect for the $2.5–5k/mo Enterprise pitch and the investor deck.

Accessibility

Iris #8F7FF7 = 6.3:1 (AA body, comfortably AAA large), gold #F5B544 = 11.1:1. Caveat: gold and amber-warning are siblings — premium vs warning must be disambiguated by icon and context, not hue alone.

Verified green inside it

The purest semantics possible: emerald is the only green in the entire system, used exclusively for verified/positive. When green appears, it can mean exactly one thing. The trade-off: green never gets brand-level airtime.

D

Emerald Ink

The full commitment: if verification is the moat, make verification-green the brand itself. Jade leads, glacier blue supports. Requires a strict rule to keep "brand green" and "verified green" from colliding — defined below.

Bold thesis · needs discipline

Psychology

Green-led = proof, growth, "go." The brand stops describing verification and embodies it. Closest spiritual neighbour: Spotify's total ownership of one green — memorable precisely because nobody else dares.

Differentiation

High — no hiring platform is green-led. But green-on-dark is heavily colonised by crypto/trading dashboards (Robinhood energy), which can cut against "trust layer" for a conservative HR buyer.

Accessibility

Jade #2EE6A8 = 12.5:1 (the best primary contrast of any option), glacier #60A5FA = 8.0:1. The Verified Seal uses white on deep emerald #047857 = 5.5:1 — AA-clean.

Verified green inside it

The collision risk, solved by treatment: brand jade is always tints, strokes and text — never a solid fill. The Verified Seal is the ONLY solid-green element in the product: deep emerald #047857 fill, white text, shield check. Filled green = verified, full stop.
